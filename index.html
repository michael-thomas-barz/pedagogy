Hidden Phenomena

One of the most wonderful parts of mathematics is observing the interesting phenomena of the mathematical world. And, historically, it seems humans have found the most interesting phenomena when in pursuit of solutions to concrete mathematical problems. The goal of this blog is to use problems as a way to show off interesting phenomena which, without the problem guiding our attention, would be hidden from view.
